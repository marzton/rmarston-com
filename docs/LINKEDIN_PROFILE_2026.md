## About
I build systems where infrastructure, software, publishing, research, automation, vendors, data, and human judgment have to work as one operating model.

My path into systems architecture was not a straight institutional ladder. It began with design and drafting, moved through manufacturing and technical documentation, expanded into web development and B2B publishing operations, and then into cloud infrastructure, identity, automation, applied AI, research tooling, and product architecture. The common thread is systems thinking: understand the dependencies, reduce ambiguity, make the interfaces explicit, and leave behind something another person can operate.

That path also made me highly self-directed. I grew up in a large foster/adoptive family as one of eight siblings, where cooperation, trust, contribution, and shared responsibility were everyday operating requirements rather than leadership theory. I learned the difference between doing everything alone and being accountable for making sure the work gets done. I am comfortable owning difficult work independently, bringing in specialists when needed, challenging weak assumptions, documenting decisions, and translating between technical and business contexts.

I use AI and automation aggressively, but I do not outsource judgment to them. Model output can extend analysis and execution; people still own verification, risk, and the final decision.

Current work through Gold Shore includes systems architecture, Cloudflare-centered infrastructure, AI/LLM workflows, automation, technical operations, research systems, risk intelligence, publishing concepts, and evidence/provenance tooling. Earlier work includes WordPress/PHP publishing platforms, subscriber-data migrations, digital advertising and analytics, hosting/DNS operations, client infrastructure, e-commerce, CAD, and manufacturing documentation.

I am most useful where the problem is real, the boundaries are unclear, and the organization needs someone who can connect architecture, operations, documentation, vendors, and accountability into one durable system.
