Perspective

A nontraditional path is useful only when it produces better judgment, stronger ownership, and evidence of real work. The objective is not to reject institutions or credentials. It is to know what each can and cannot substitute for: curiosity, trust, repetition, accountability, and the ability to make a system hold together when the instructions are incomplete.
